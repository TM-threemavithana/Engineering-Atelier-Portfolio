## Research Overview

A deterministic, parameter-driven Computer Vision and Image Processing (CVIP) research prototype investigating the authentication of Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR) banknotes across LKR 500, 1,000, and 5,000 denominations. Developed in collaboration with Asitha0012 (repository owner) and udaraDev (contributor).

## Core Architecture

1. **Stage 1 (Automatic Geometric Alignment):** Canny edge detection (75/200), contour-based perspective rectification, Normalized Cross-Correlation (NCC) coarse orientation detection, and ORB fine homography (5,000 keypoints, RANSAC 5.0px tolerance) with Lanczos4 interpolation.
2. **Stage 2 (Visual Feature Verification `F1`–`F7`):** Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE), structural similarity (SSIM), and 2D HSV color histogram correlation.
3. **Stage 3 (Morphological Feature Verification `F8`–`F12`):** Tactile blind dot contour counting, asymmetric serial number stepwise height verification, vertical red serial HSV masking, windowed security thread segment-merging metrology (2.5mm / 3.0mm), and directional tactile edge line counting.
4. **Stage 4 (Two-Stage Decision Layer):** 
   - **Stage A (Conjunctive Veto Gate):** `V_StageA = F1 ∧ F7 ∧ F11` — instant counterfeit rejection if any critical unforgeable security feature fails.
   - **Stage B (Flat Voting):** `P_total = (1/12) * ∑(i=1..12) Fi ≥ 0.75` — requires at least 75% pass rate across all features to absorb natural paper degradation.

## Reported Experimental Performance (Pending Independent Reproducibility Checks)

*All figures below represent findings reported in the research manuscript. They are not yet marked as independently reproduced because the full 3,000-image dataset and exact 750-image evaluation split manifest are not yet independently accessible.*

- **Manuscript-Reported Held-Out Test Performance (Reported N=750):** 96.67% accuracy, 96.71% F1-score, 98.13% recall, 95.20% specificity (TP: 368, TN: 357, FP: 18, FN: 7).
- **Manuscript-Reported Latency:** 0.49 s cached CPU inference per banknote on a standard single-thread Intel Core i5 without GPU acceleration.
- **Manuscript-Reported Ablation:** Removing the Veto Gate and relying on flat voting yielded reported accuracies of 88.40% (66.7% threshold), 92.10% (75% threshold), and 89.70% (83.3% threshold), with the Two-Stage framework achieving 96.67%.

## Honest Research Boundaries & Scientific Limitations

- **RGB-Only Capture:** Optical RGB imaging cannot authenticate physical substrate properties verified by transmitted light, UV fluorescence, or infrared absorption.
- **Synthetic Counterfeit Evaluation:** Evaluated on procedurally generated adversarial counterfeits simulating localized feature corruptions; full physical counterfeit specimens require specialized hardware sensing.
- **Dataset Partitioning & Data Leakage Risk:** The manuscript reports a random 75/25 split of augmented images. Full scientific verification of zero data leakage requires confirming that augmentations of the same physical banknote did not cross the calibration/test boundary.
- **Legal & Regulatory Clearance:** Reproduction of currency images is governed by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka Act No. 16 of 2023, Section 55. All currency images and feature crops remain strictly excluded from public portfolio assets pending regulatory permission.
