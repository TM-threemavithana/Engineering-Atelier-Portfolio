## Research Overview

A deterministic, parameter-driven Computer Vision and Image Processing (CVIP) research prototype investigating the authentication of Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR) banknotes across LKR 500, 1,000, and 5,000 denominations. Developed in collaborative partnership with Asitha0012 (repository owner) and udaraDev (contributor). Individual role confirmed as Computer Vision & Research Software Development — Core Contributor, without formal project leadership or sole authorship claims.

## Core Architecture

1. **Stage 1 (Automatic Geometric Alignment):** Canny edge detection (75/200), contour-based perspective rectification, Normalized Cross-Correlation (NCC) coarse orientation detection, and ORB fine homography (5,000 keypoints, RANSAC 5.0px tolerance) with Lanczos4 interpolation.
2. **Stage 2 (Visual Feature Verification `F1`–`F7`):** Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE), structural similarity (SSIM), and 2D HSV color histogram correlation.
3. **Stage 3 (Morphological Feature Verification `F8`–`F12`):** Tactile blind dot contour counting, asymmetric serial number stepwise height verification, vertical red serial HSV masking, windowed security thread segment-merging metrology (2.5mm / 3.0mm), and directional tactile edge line counting.
4. **Stage 4 (Two-Stage Decision Layer):**
   - **Stage A (Conjunctive Veto Gate):** `V_StageA = F1 ∧ F7 ∧ F11` — instant counterfeit rejection if any critical unforgeable security feature fails.
   - **Stage B (Flat Voting):** `P_total = (1/12) * ∑(i=1..12) Fi ≥ 0.75` — requires at least 75% pass rate across all features to absorb natural paper degradation.

## Reported Experimental Performance (Pending Independent Reproducibility Checks)

_All figures below represent findings reported in the research manuscript. They are classified as manuscript-reported findings, not independently reproduced results, and must not be presented as independent real-world counterfeit validation._

- **Manuscript-Reported Classification Evaluation (Nominally Held-Out N=750):** 96.67% accuracy, 96.71% F1-score, 98.13% recall, 95.20% specificity (TP: 368, TN: 357, FP: 18, FN: 7) evaluated on 750 augmented images.
- **Manuscript-Reported Latency Benchmark:** 0.49 s cached CPU inference per banknote (averaged over 100 runs) on a standard single-thread Intel Core i5 without GPU acceleration, profiled as a separate latency benchmark from the classification test.
- **Manuscript-Reported Ablation Findings:** Removing the Veto Gate and relying on flat voting yielded reported accuracies of 88.40% (66.7% threshold), 92.10% (75% threshold), and 89.70% (83.3% threshold), compared to the Two-Stage framework's reported 96.67%.

## Honest Research Boundaries & Scientific Limitations

- **Synthetic Counterfeit Evaluation:** Evaluated using procedurally generated synthetic counterfeit images simulating localized feature corruptions; does not constitute independent real-world counterfeit validation.
- **Procedural Generation Framework:** Contribution centered on developing the automated procedural synthetic-counterfeit generation framework; the complete 3,000-image dataset and exact 750-image evaluation manifest are not claimed as publicly downloadable without a verified distribution link.
- **RGB-Only Capture:** Optical RGB imaging cannot authenticate physical substrate properties verified by transmitted light, UV fluorescence, or infrared absorption.
- **Dataset Partitioning & Data Leakage Risk:** Possible data leakage between augmented images originating from the same physical banknote due to missing note-identity grouping evidence in the 75/25 split.
- **Independent Reproduction Required:** Results require independent reproduction in an isolated test harness and additional real-world validation against physical counterfeits.
